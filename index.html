About CALIX

Crafted with precision.
Delivered with care.

CALIX Glass and Aluminum Fabrication is a single proprietorship established in 2017, specializing in glass and aluminum fabrication, project planning, and construction management.

We pair quality materials with practical design standards and client-centered service—creating solutions that suit the project, the space, and the people who use it.
